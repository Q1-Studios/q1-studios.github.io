About Us

We're a small team of passionate computer science students building games in their free time.

Cucu

Coding Music Sound

Cucu is a botanical wonder, running entirely on spite and way too little sleep. When he gets excited about something, nothing except for the laws of physics (i.e. hitting him with a brick) can stop him. Cucu also composes music and beats his expensive microphone up with a stick for the perfect clicking sound.

Seal

Art Coding

50% 3D Artist, 50% Mad Scientist, 100% Seal. Boasting natural buoyancy and a skill set in 3D Art that rivals Leonardo da Vinci (if da Vinci was a god-tier shitposter), Seal never backs down from tackling a challenging project. At Q1, he's a force to be reckoned with, pushing both visuals and collateral damage to their maximum.

Wolphi

Coding

Wolphi wrangles both frontends and backends without fear. He's the only real industry veteran at Q1 who has already excessively experienced The Horrors™ of employment, and we're preeeetty sure he's close to retirement. But until he gets his senior citizen card, he codes awesome stuff and supplies the team with potato goulash.

Portar

Art Coding Music Sound

If you're looking for a Swiss Army knife, look no further than Portar! Having an extensive technical background, Portar gladly takes on any challenge, from code to 2D art and 3D modeling. The fact that he is (by and large) not delusional adds a valuable trait to the Q1 team.

TobiShreds

Coding

Tobi adamantly insists that he is NOT a genetic experiment or a cyborg, but his ability to bend time at his whim clearly proves otherwise. Tobi is an absolute machine (we know he secretly speaks binary) and loves taking care of tricky code. He is also apt at creating more tricky code that will become someone else's problem.

Xtoph

Coding

Once bright-eyed and bushy-tailed, Xtoph came into closer contact with programming war crimes than any person ever should. Although the spark in his eyes has been replaced by the weariness of a grizzled veteran, his passion for learning and dedication persisted. As a part of Q1, he now gets a chance to be on the offending side of Geneva convention violations.
